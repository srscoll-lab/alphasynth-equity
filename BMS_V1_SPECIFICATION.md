# AlphaSynth Business Momentum Score — BMS V1

**Status:** FROZEN
**Version:** BMS V1
**Purpose:** Deterministic identification and tracking of changes in company business fundamentals.

## Frozen BMS V1 Components

1. Five-factor BMS structure
2. Factor weights: Earnings 25%, Economics 25%, Execution 25%, Balance Sheet 15%, Management Delivery 10%
3. Existing deterministic BMS/TCS calculation
4. Existing evidence logic
5. Current Fundamental Momentum: Strong Positive / Positive / Neutral / Negative / Strong Negative
6. Momentum Stage: Watch / Emerging / Sustained / Fading
7. BUILDING + ESTABLISHED are shown as SUSTAINED
8. fading_warning takes precedence and is shown as FADING
9. Gemini is explanation-only and does not calculate or alter BMS
10. BMS is a measure of change in business fundamentals, not company quality, valuation or investment recommendation

## Lifecycle Definitions

**WATCH:** Evidence worth monitoring, but stronger lifecycle confirmation is not yet present.
**EMERGING:** A meaningful new positive fundamental inflection has appeared.
**SUSTAINED:** Positive fundamental momentum has persisted beyond the initial inflection.
**FADING:** Previously meaningful positive momentum has deteriorated materially.

## Validation Snapshot

Current 154-company lifecycle distribution:
- Watch: 105
- Emerging: 10
- Sustained: 29
- Fading: 10

Historical return testing is limited and must not be overstated.
The available matched 120-day sample contained 46 Sustained and 4 Emerging observations, with no Watch or Fading observations.
Sustained showed approximately +9.36% mean 120-day relative return, +6.14% median, and 58.7% beat-Nifty rate.
