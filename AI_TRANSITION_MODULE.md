# AlphaSynth AI Transition Observatory

**Status:** Pilot contract 0.1  
**Initial scope:** 12 Indian listed IT companies across services, ER&D and product/platform models.

## Purpose

The module asks two different questions:

1. **AI disruption exposure:** How vulnerable is the existing revenue model to automation, pricing pressure and client insourcing?
2. **AI adaptation readiness:** Is the company producing verifiable economic evidence of a move toward platforms, outcomes, domain-led integration or AI-enabled revenue?

These assessments are an overlay. They do not alter BMS V1 scores or lifecycle classifications.

## Evidence policy

Scores may use only dated company results, exchange filings, earnings transcripts, investor presentations and annual reports. AI or management marketing language is not itself monetisation evidence. Missing dimensions remain `null`, never zero.

Every record is labelled either:

- `point_in_time`: uses only material published on or before the assessment date and may enter the backtest; or
- `reconstructed_today`: a present-day historical reconstruction for explanation, excluded from unbiased backtesting.

At least 60% of the weighted dimensions on both sides must be observable before a classification is published.

## Historical validation

The first study window begins with FY2023 disclosures. Each quarterly assessment will be frozen and evaluated over the following 3, 6 and 12 months against:

- revenue growth, operating margin and revenue per employee;
- headcount and utilisation where disclosed;
- platform, licence, outcome-based or AI-linked revenue where disclosed;
- management delivery against dated commitments;
- total return, drawdown and valuation change relative to the appropriate IT business-model cohort.

Market performance is an outcome, not proof that AI caused the outcome.

## Expansion rule

The complete IT universe will be catalogued after the pilot is calibrated. Companies without adequate disclosure remain visible as `insufficient_evidence`; they are not assigned artificial zeroes or low-confidence rankings.
