## Video follow-up / reply

Here’s a short walkthrough. Momentum shows what the price is doing; the Fundamental Change Score looks at changes in the business. They answer different questions.

Existing FCS reports are available to read. New reviews can take up to 15 minutes and may finish without a score if the evidence is insufficient. Lifecycle needs three comparable quarterly scores; it is dated and isn’t available for every company.

This is a research beta—not a stock-tip service or a promise of returns.
